Polish normative acts and their official texts are not subject to copyright (art. 4 of the Act on Copyright and
Related Rights), so the text is kept in the repository: tests and the eval set run on exactly these bytes.

The text has two versions of some articles: the one in force until 4 November 2026 and the one from
5 November 2026 (art. 18³ᵃ–18³ᵍ, 94³, 94³ᵃ). The parser reads the dates from the footnotes.
